# Showcase and final reflection

## Gallery walk

Half the class shows their projects at their computers. The other half visits. After 15 minutes, swap.

When you visit:

- Try **at least one input the author did not plan for**.
- Leave written feedback: one thing that works well, and one idea.

## Final self-assessment

1. Compare your **week 7** confidence log with your recent entries. What changed?
2. Rate the year's "I can" statements, each with evidence.
3. Compare your **week 24** rating ("How sure am I that I can build this?") with how the project actually went.
4. Write a short note to next year's Grade 9: **"What I know now about how I learn programming."**
