# Design before code

**Project, session 2 of 5.** Today you design. **No code** this session.

## Your teacher's feedback

Read the feedback on your proposal. Write down **one change** you will make.

## 1. Design

Make a design on paper with three parts:

1. **The main flow:** pseudocode or a flowchart. Use `ASK`, `SAY`, `IF`/`ELSE`, `WHILE` and `FOR`, like in week 11.
2. **Your functions:** for each one, its name, what it receives (parameters) and what it returns.

| Function | Receives | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `ask_number(question)` | the question text | a whole number |
| | | |

3. **Your data:** what does the program remember? Which lists (or tuples) do you need?

## 2. Swap and trace

Give your design and your test plan to another student. They **trace** your design with your tests, like in week 11, and mark exactly where it breaks.

## 3. Revise

Fix the problems the trace revealed.

## Before you leave

Complete in your log:

*"The riskiest part of my design is ___. If it fails, my plan B is ___."*
